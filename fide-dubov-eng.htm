About the FIDE Dubov System

The Dubov Swiss Pairing System (FIDE Handbook C.04.4.1, effective February 2026) is designed to maximise fair opposition by equalising the Average Rating of Opponents (ARO) of all players in each scoregroup.

Key Dubov System Principles:

  • Upfloaters only: Unlike the Dutch system, there are no downfloaters. Scoregroups requiring opponents draw upfloaters from lower scoregroups.
  • ARO Equalization: Players with lower ARO are paired with higher-rated opponents.
  • Subgroups G1 and G2: G1 contains White seekers (sorted by ascending ARO); G2 contains Black seekers (sorted by ascending TPN).
  • Middle-out shifter rule (Art. 4.3.3): When subgroup sizes must be equalized, candidates are shifted from the middle of the spectrum outwards.
  • Strict Color Rules ([C3]): Two players with the same absolute color preference can never meet.
  • Application tie-breaks: This tournament configuration ranks players by Points, Buchholz Cut 1, Sonneborn-Berger, Buchholz and TPN. The selected tie-break order is a tournament regulation, not part of the Dubov pairing procedure itself.

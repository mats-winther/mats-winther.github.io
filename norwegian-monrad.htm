Monrad Tournament Manager

Pairing and administration of tournaments according to the Norwegian Chess Federation’s Monrad system

Tournament setup

Players

Enter one player per line using Name;Club;Rating. Tabs and commas are also accepted. Club and rating may be omitted. Lines beginning with # are ignored.

Seeded draw

For a seeded draw, players are ranked by rating and divided into four groups: A, B, C and D. Players are then drawn in the repeating group order:

A, C, D, B, C, A, B, D

If the player count is not divisible by four, the lowest-ranked remainder is drawn randomly and assigned the final start numbers.

Printable round lists

A frozen copy of the round list is stored before each round is paired. It can be printed later even after the tournament has moved to another round.

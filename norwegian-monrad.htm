Monrad Tournament Manager

Pairing and administration according to the Norwegian Chess Federation's Monrad system

Tournament setup

Players

Enter one player per line using Name;Club;Rating. Tabs and commas are also accepted. Quoted CSV fields are supported. Club and rating may be omitted. Lines beginning with # are ignored.

Seeded draw

Players are ranked by rating and divided into four equal groups: A, B, C and D. Players are drawn using the repeating group order:

A, C, D, B, C, A, B, D

If the number of players is not divisible by four, the lowest-ranked remainder is drawn randomly and receives the final start numbers.

Adjourned games

Printable round lists

A frozen copy of the round list is stored before each round is paired. It can be printed after the tournament has moved to another round.
